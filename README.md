## Football stats and historical weather data

This is a project about combining football stats with historical weather data to support several use cases. Suggested use cases below:


|Use Case|Target Audience|What should Gold show|
|--------|---------------|---------------------|
|Football Analytics|Trainer team and analytics|Pass accuracy in varied weather. Playstyle in varied weather. Pass length in varied weather.|
|Match Outcome Analytics|Football Analytics|Goals and results in different weather conditions.|
|Stadium Weather Analytics|Event Organizers & Stadium Operators|Which stadiums have the most challenging weather? Maybe bad for pitch with too much rain or too much sun?|
|Potentially Stadium Attendance Analytics|Club, Stadium Owner, League|Does the attendance vary by weather?|
|Referee Decisions Analytics|Referee comitee|Does different wheater effect number of yellow and red cards given? Will the referee give more cards in rainy wheater?|
